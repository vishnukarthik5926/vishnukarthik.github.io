About Me

The story of a curious mind turning ideas into reality

👨‍💻

Hi, I'm Vishnu Karthik — a B.Tech 3rd year ECE student (Batch 2024–2028) at Vignan Foundation for Science, Technology & Research (VFSTR), Vadlamudi, Guntur. I believe every idea deserves to become reality. Welcome to my corner of the internet where I document my journey from concepts to working projects.

As a student, I started with curiosity — the kind that makes you wonder "how does this work?" and "what if I could build that?" That curiosity led me down a rabbit hole of electronics, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and web development.

Today, I build things. Some work on the first try. Most don't. But every failed attempt teaches me something new, and I share those lessons here because learning in public makes the journey more meaningful.

My Philosophy

🔬

Learn by Doing

Tutorials can teach theory, but real learning happens when you build something and break it.

📢

Share the Process

Documenting failures is as valuable as showcasing successes. The journey matters.

🤝

AI as a Partner

AI doesn't replace learning — it accelerates it. Use it wisely, understand deeply.

My Journey

2023

The Spark

Discovered the world of Arduino and electronics. Built first embedded projects, got completely hooked on hardware and the idea of making physical devices respond to code.

2024

Going Deeper

Moved to ESP32, STM32, and IoT systems. Built a Smart Weather Monitoring system that won 1st Prize at VFSTR Engineering Day Expo. Joined B.Tech ECE at VFSTR, Guntur. Won 1st Prize at Space Tech Ideathon. Explored robotics, RF, and sensor fusion.

2025

AI Integration & Rocketry

Built TARA AI assistant, gesture-controlled robot, and Universal RF + Mecanum vehicle. Team selected for IN-SPACe National Rocketry Challenge. Started CubiSense hardware project. Integrated Gemini API, Claude, and OpenRouter into real firmware workflows.

2026

Ideas2RealityWithVK

Launched Ideas2RealityWithVK while in 3rd year. Building in public, advancing the Vehicle Event Recorder, pushing the CanSat CDR for IN-SPACe, and connecting with fellow makers and builders around the world.
